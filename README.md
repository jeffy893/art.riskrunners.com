# Risk Runners Art Captive

A captive insurance feasibility study for art businesses — rendered as a live website at [art.riskrunners.com](https://art.riskrunners.com).

## What Is This?

This is a demonstration of how a Protected Cell Company (PCC) captive insurance syndicate could serve 12 art businesses — galleries, private collectors, art shippers, and conservation studios — pooling fortuitous risk instead of paying unrecoverable premiums to commercial carriers.

## Covered Segments

- **Galleries** — Exhibition, installation, transit, and public access risks
- **Private Collectors** — Storage, lending, theft, and environmental damage
- **Art Shippers** — Transit damage, climate control, handling, and logistics
- **Conservation Studios** — Treatment complications, chemical reactions, custody liability

## Important Disclaimers

- This is a **feasibility study**, not a regulatory filing or insurance product
- All policies, entities, and scenarios are **simulated**
- Formation requires validation by a **licensed actuary** and **qualified captive manager**
- Coverage applies strictly to **fortuitous risk**; speculative risk is expressly excluded
- Compliance analysis references Arizona Revised Statutes but has not been formally reviewed
